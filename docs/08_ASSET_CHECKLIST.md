# 08 — Photo and Video Asset Checklist

## Media strategy
Dev is more comfortable in candid moments and with loved ones than in heavily posed solo photos. The site should lean into that. Do not force a conventional photoshoot aesthetic.

## Suggested first batch
Aim for:
- 2–3 solo photos that Dev genuinely likes.
- 2–3 candid/social photos.
- 1–2 creative/work-related photos.
- 2–5 short videos: dancing, enjoying a moment, or spontaneous creative clips.
- 1 optional video poster for the hero.

This is a guide, not a quota. Use fewer assets if they are stronger.

## Good content categories
- Dancing / Bollywood / freestyle.
- Music, guitar or DJ learning.
- Cafe visits.
- City exploration and travel.
- Creative/design moments.
- A natural portrait.
- A fun candid moment.

## Video preparation
- Prefer 5–20 second clips.
- Trim dead time.
- Export compressed MP4 (H.264) or WebM.
- Keep audio muted by default.
- Create a poster image.
- Use `playsInline`, `muted` and `preload="none"` or an appropriate lazy strategy.
- Avoid autoplaying multiple videos.
- Do not put a large video background on the entire page.

## Photo preparation
- Prefer WebP/AVIF for web delivery.
- Keep a high-quality original backup.
- Crop with the subject's face/body in mind.
- Avoid aggressive beauty filters or edits.
- Ensure the image still looks good at mobile width.

## Suggested file naming
Use simple, descriptive filenames:
- `dev-portrait-01.webp`
- `dev-dance-01.mp4`
- `dev-dance-01-poster.webp`
- `dev-cafe-01.webp`
- `dev-travel-01.webp`

## Privacy and consent
- Do not publish private photos or videos of friends/family without their permission.
- Avoid images showing addresses, vehicle registration plates, private documents, screens, phone numbers or other identifying details.
- Do not include children in public media without appropriate guardian permission.
- Do not upload intimate or sensitive media.
- Review metadata/location information where relevant before publishing.

## Placeholder plan
Build the site using clearly labelled placeholders first. Replace them only after Dev has selected the final media. Never use random stock people as if they were Dev.

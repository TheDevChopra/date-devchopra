# 09 — Privacy and Publishing Rules

## Data minimisation
The first version should be a static personal website. Do not collect visitor information unless there is a clear, approved reason.

## Do not publish
- Home address or precise live location.
- Personal phone number unless Dev explicitly approves it.
- Financial account details, balances, income, investment amounts or property specifics.
- Private family or friend information.
- Sensitive personal conversations.
- Unapproved photos/videos of other people.
- Any secrets, credentials, API keys or private work information.

## Contact
If adding a contact CTA:
- Dev must choose the channel and approve the exact destination.
- Make it optional.
- Do not ask visitors to share personal or sensitive details.
- Do not create a public database of submissions by default.
- If a form is later added, explain what is collected, why, how it is used and how it can be deleted.

## Faith and values
Dev has said Hinduism is important to him and he respects other religions. Represent this as a personal value, not as a judgement of visitors or other communities.

## Relationship tone
- Serious long-term intentions can be communicated honestly.
- Do not imply that the visitor is already a partner or owes Dev a response.
- “Potential Future Wife” is an intentionally bold, humorous greeting; the rest of the site should make the tone light and welcoming.
- Do not use guilt, jealousy, pressure, countdowns or manipulative engagement tactics.
- Do not frame possessiveness as control or entitlement. Emphasise trust, care and respect.
- Do not publicly rank visitors or publish a partner eligibility checklist.

## Accessibility and safety
- Respect reduced-motion settings.
- Keep text readable.
- Provide keyboard-accessible controls.
- Do not autoplay sound.
- Avoid flashing effects.
- Ensure links clearly identify their destination.

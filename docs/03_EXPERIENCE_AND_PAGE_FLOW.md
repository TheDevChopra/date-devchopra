# 03 — Experience and Page Flow

## Overall experience
A vertical, scroll-led interactive story. The site should feel like a blend of a romantic digital scrapbook, a playful mini-game and a personal landing page.

Keep the experience short enough to explore in 2–5 minutes. Do not force the visitor to complete every interaction.

## 0. Loading / first impression
- Lightweight loading state with a small flower or sparkle motif.
- Avoid a long fake loading percentage.
- Preload only the first-screen assets.

## 1. Flower entrance
Full-screen, mobile-first intro.
- Soft warm background.
- Flower petals drift down from the top.
- A few petals rotate gently and move at different speeds.
- Opening text: **“Hey, Potential Future Wife.”**
- Supporting line: “Well, this is an interesting way to meet, isn't it?”
- Primary button: “Enter Dev's little universe”.
- On tap, petals scatter/fade and the main page appears.
- Provide a visible skip/continue option; do not make the visitor wait.

## 2. Meet Dev
- Short introduction with one strong portrait or candid video still.
- Suggested copy: “Hi, I'm Dev. A little romantic, a little chaotic, and usually thinking about 12 things at once.”
- Use a short looping silent clip only if it is optimised and has a poster image.
- Avoid a long biography.

## 3. The personality mix
Use 4 compact interactive cards:
- Romantic.
- Funny.
- Ambitious.
- Social / creative.

Each card has one short line and a small interaction (flip, reveal, hover on desktop, tap on mobile). Do not hide essential content behind hover.

## 4. The playlist of me
A playful section for:
- Qawwali.
- Hip-hop.
- Pop.
- Guitar learning.
- DJ learning.
- Bollywood / freestyle dancing.

Use animated cassette, music player or tracklist motifs. Do not stream copyrighted music or embed copyrighted tracks without permission. Use text labels, original sound effects or links to official listening platforms if desired. Audio must never autoplay.

## 5. Green flags, red flags & terms
- Two-column desktop layout; stacked cards on mobile.
- Self-aware jokes, not a serious scorecard.
- Use “green-ish” and “red-ish” if it makes the tone warmer.
- Batman gets a small badge or comic-panel treatment.
- Add a note that care does not mean control and that trust/respect matter.

## 6. Date.exe
A scroll-triggered timeline or horizontal card sequence:
1. Pickup (only if mutually comfortable).
2. Connaught Place or Hauz Khas.
3. Walk and talk.
4. Cafe + lunch.
5. Music-filled drive.
6. Drop-off and goodbye.
Little surprises appear as easter eggs between steps.
On small screens, use a vertical timeline or swipeable cards; do not require horizontal scrolling of the whole page.

## 7. The ambition corner
Keep this concise:
- “Started working at 15.”
- “Paid my own college fees and many expenses.”
- “Growth Marketing Engineer at Veena Studio, working on AI music production.”
- “Long game: build companies that solve real problems.”
Do not publish salary, investment balances, property plans or other private financial details.

## 8. The visual scrapbook
- Photos and short videos.
- Captions should be short and playful.
- Use candid moments, dance clips, creative moments and a few photos with loved ones only where everyone shown has consented.
- Do not overfill the gallery. Start with 6–10 strong assets.
- Add a “more stories are better told in person” line.

## 9. Tiny compatibility quiz
Optional, for fun only. No data storage by default.
- 3–5 playful questions.
- Examples: “Pick the date soundtrack”, “Coffee or hot chocolate?”, “Walk and talk or sit and talk?”
- Result should be a playful response, not a score or judgement.
- Include “This quiz is scientifically unserious.”
- Do not ask for religion, age, address, phone number, relationship history or other sensitive details.
- Do not block the rest of the site if the quiz is skipped.

## 10. Closing
- Copy: “Anyway, that's a little about me. The better stories are for an actual conversation.”
- CTA: “Say hello” / “Let's get coffee”.
- Link only to social or messaging accounts Dev explicitly chooses to publish.
- If WhatsApp is used, use a public contact link only after Dev approves it. Consider Instagram or email as alternatives.
- No forced pop-up, no fake urgency, no “you owe me a reply”.

## Navigation
- Prefer a small floating progress indicator or subtle section labels.
- Avoid a large sticky nav on mobile.
- Include a discreet “Back to top”.
- Respect browser back and normal scrolling.

## Easter eggs
Optional ideas:
- A tiny bat signal appears after the visitor taps Batman.
- A flower follows the cursor on desktop only.
- A hidden “DJ loading…” sticker.
- A tiny coffee cup wiggle when the cafe is mentioned.
- A surprise message after the final CTA is tapped.
All easter eggs must be optional, accessible and non-blocking.

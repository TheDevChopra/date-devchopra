# 01 — Product Brief

## Working title
**The Dev Experience™**

## Domain
`date.devchopra.life`

## Product vision
Build a playful, romantic, highly interactive personal website that introduces Dev to a potential romantic connection. It should feel like opening a small, thoughtfully designed digital world: warm, funny, flirty, creative, and distinctly personal.

It is not a conventional portfolio. It is not a dating platform. It is not a complete personal biography.

## Primary outcome
A visitor should:
1. Smile within the first few seconds.
2. Understand Dev's broad personality and intentions.
3. Find one or two details that make them curious.
4. Feel invited, not pressured, to start a conversation.

## Target visitor
A woman who has received the link or discovered the site and wants to know a little about Dev.

Assume she is viewing it on a phone. The experience must be excellent on small screens, work with touch, and load quickly.

## Personality blend
- Romantic and affectionate.
- Funny, flirty, sarcastic, smooth and occasionally cheesy.
- Extroverted and sociable; recently becoming more outgoing.
- Creative, curious, ambitious and career-focused.
- Enjoys music, dance, technology, design, films, reading and meeting people.
- Values faith, family, respect, dignity and long-term commitment.

## Product principles
- **Curiosity over completeness:** never reveal every story.
- **Personality over polish alone:** animation must support the jokes and emotional tone.
- **Playful, not pushy:** no pressure, guilt, manipulation or assumed consent.
- **Romantic, not childish:** soft visual language with mature typography and restrained decoration.
- **Mobile-first:** design for a 360px-wide phone before expanding.
- **Fast by default:** avoid heavy video backgrounds, giant animation libraries for tiny effects, and unnecessary backend complexity.
- **Honest:** do not invent achievements, preferences, relationship history or promises.

## Success signals
- The opening makes visitors want to tap.
- Visitors scroll through several sections.
- The gallery makes Dev feel real and approachable.
- The ending provides a simple, optional way to say hello.
- The site feels memorable without being overwhelming.

## Non-goals
Do not build:
- User accounts or sign-up.
- A swipe/match/dating system.
- Public comments or a public visitor directory.
- A personality test that claims to determine real compatibility.
- A long autobiography or full career portfolio.
- A public form asking visitors for sensitive personal information.
- A fake “application” that stores or judges women's answers.

## Core line
**The website introduces Dev. The conversation discovers Dev.**
